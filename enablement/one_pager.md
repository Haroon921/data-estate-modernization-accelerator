## Data Estate Modernization - Partner One-Pager

**Pitch**: Move SQL and Synapse workloads to a supported, cheaper, AI-ready platform - with a repeatable path.

**Customer signals**: SQL 2016 or older, upcoming renewal, high infra/ops cost, Synapse pools under-used, "we want AI but data is a mess".

**Offer stack**: (1) 2-week assessment -> (2) business case -> (3) migration wave 1 -> (4) mirror + governance -> (5) AI use case on Gold data.

**Discovery questions**: What SQL versions and when do support/renewal dates hit? Which workloads have instance-level dependencies? What does Synapse cost vs usage today? Where does data for reporting/AI come from?

**Objections**: "Migration is risky" -> validation framework + parallel run. "Too costly" -> Hybrid Benefit + reservations in the business case. "Not ready for AI" -> mirroring gives a low-effort start.

**Assets**: assessment scripts, router, calculator, Bicep, runbooks, validation, workshop.
